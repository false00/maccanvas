Independent AAC-to-WAVE audio converter
=====================================

MacCanvas-audio-windows-x64.exe is a separate, independently usable program.
It reads standard LOAS/LATM from stdin and writes streaming PCM WAVE to stdout.
It is not linked into, or embedded in, the AGPL-licensed MacCanvas viewer.
The programs exchange standard media through private pipes.
